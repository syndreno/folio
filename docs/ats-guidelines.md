# ATS Readiness checks

Folio provides an advisory ATS Readiness score. It cannot predict a specific employer's parser, ranking rules, recruiter decisions, or interview outcomes.

The score is a weighted summary of 34 checks. A failed check never blocks editing or export.

## Contact

- Full name
- Professional title
- Valid email
- Phone number
- Location
- Safe, readable web links
- Contact icons accompanied by ordinary text

## Sections

- Visible Experience section
- Visible Skills section
- Visible Education section
- Commonly recognized headings
- No visible placeholder or empty sections

## Content

- At least five relevant skills
- Scannable experience bullets
- Measurable achievements
- Reasonably concise paragraphs, descriptions, and bullets
- Limited excessive word repetition
- Resume-style voice without first-person pronouns

## Dates

- Dates on relevant entries
- Conventional month/year or year-only formats
- Consistent formatting throughout the resume

Dates can be detected in structured metadata and, for imported Markdown, in entry descriptions or titles.

## Design

- Body font of at least 10 pt
- Broadly compatible font family
- Readable line spacing
- Sufficient body-text contrast
- Single-column ATS layout
- No dependency on a profile photo
- Non-blocking estimated page-length guidance
- No emoji, private-use glyphs, or replacement characters

## Export

- Selectable PDF text
- Logical section reading order
- No rasterized body text
- Automatic multi-page pagination
- No tables, floating text boxes, or complex columns for critical content

PDF behavior also has an automated extraction test that creates a multi-page fixture and checks that text from the first and final entries remains extractable.
